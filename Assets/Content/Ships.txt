# Ship archetypes and the equipment they fly with (plan task 1.1; ADR-009; blueprint ch. 02 §3.7, §4).
#
# Units, SI throughout:
#   mass kg; inertia kg m^2 per ship axis (pitch X, yaw Y, roll Z)
#   drag, angulardrag: force per unit of speed, kg/s and N m s per radian
#   thrust, force, strafe: N; torque: N m per axis
#   reverse: the share of thrust a negative throttle gives
#   charge, chargetime: s; maxbank: degrees
#   capacity, charge, drain, cruisepower: units of a pool, and units per second
#   docking: the docking points the hull may use, berth, medium or large (ch. 04 §2; plan task 2.2)
#   shield: the shield generator; capacity and regen in shield points, draw from the power pool a second,
#     offline the share of capacity it collapses at, rebuild the seconds it then stays down (ch. 03 §3.5)
#   launcher: refire s, power per shot, speed m/s, lifetime s; damage and energydamage as the blueprint's
#     hull and energy damage (a shield takes half the first and all the second); carve the energy a hit
#     spends removing voxels, in material strength units, with its shape, radius and length in cells
#     (ADR-012, ADR-014). The carve's numbers are whole: it is integer arithmetic.
#   mount: a launcher in a weapon module of the ship's hull (Assets/Hulls/Hulls.txt); its muzzle is the
#     middle of the module's forward face
#
# The two escorts carry the blueprint's first-tier light fighter as authored: top speed 80 m/s,
# thruster 200 m/s, strafe 33 m/s, pitch and yaw 92 degrees a second. They differ only in bank.

engine name=LightEngine thrust=48000 drag=599 reverse=0.2 chargetime=5 cruisepower=20
powerplant name=LightPowerPlant capacity=1000 charge=100 thrustcapacity=1000 thrustcharge=100
thruster name=BasicThruster force=72000 drain=165

# First tier, after the blueprint's class 1 guns (median 136 damage and 42 power a second): five shots a
# second of 28 damage for 8.5 power, 600 m/s for a second, so 600 m of range. A shield of 450, the
# blueprint's smallest, collapses at 15 % after 28 hits and refills from nothing in 45 s (ch. 03 §4).
# The carve, a capsule of radius 2 boring six cells along the shot with 400 units, was set by the
# headless duel (ADR-014): the beaten ship's core is reached within the first rebuild windows.
shield name=LightShield capacity=450 regen=10 draw=10 offline=0.15 rebuild=12
launcher name=LightBlaster refire=0.2 power=8.5 speed=600 lifetime=1 damage=28 carve=400 shape=capsule radius=2 length=6
