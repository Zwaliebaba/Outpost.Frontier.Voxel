# Outpost:Frontier — the system: its zones, its stations and where a new game starts (ADR-002;
# plan tasks 2.1 and 2.2; blueprint ch. 01 §3.4 and §3.5, ch. 04 §3.2).
#
# zone: name, shape (sphere: radius; box: size, three full edges; cylinder: radius and length along
#   its own Y), position in metres, optional rotation (yaw, pitch, roll in degrees, as an instance).
#   With instance=, the position is in that instance's VOX coordinates, as a light's is.
#   edge: the outer share of the zone across which immersion ramps from 1 to 0.
#   damage: hit points per second at full immersion; interference: scanner interference, 0 to 1.
#   region: the name a named region shows. "Smallest zone wins" decides between overlapping zones.
# station: a base the player can dock at, made of the instances whose docking points it offers.
# start: the station a new game starts docked at (GameDesign.md D18), and the repair kits a new
#   character carries (plan task 3.11).

# The frigate's breach: venting debris and a cloud that blinds sensors, around the burning hull
# section between the two fire lights.
zone name=FrigateBreach shape=sphere instance=Frigate position=37,74,14 radius=60 edge=0.5 damage=20 interference=0.6

# The shipyard and the space around it, for the location read-out.
zone name=ShipyardSpace shape=sphere position=-40,40,140 radius=1500 edge=0 region="Kessler Reach Shipyard"

station name=Shipyard label="Kessler Reach Shipyard" region=ShipyardSpace instance=Hangar instance=CollarWest instance=CollarLower

start station=Shipyard repairkits=3
