# The flight smoke run (plan tasks 1.11 and 2.8): the player's escort, E-114, launched from its berth
# and flown by its commands into the frigate's breach, where the hazard warning shows. Pixels of the
# 1920 x 1080 smoke frame; ticks are server ticks, one per frame. Keys are named as in
# Assets/Content/Bindings.txt.
#
# A new game starts docked, in the tactical view (GameDesign.md D18). Launch, clicked in the Services
# inspector; the station carries the ship out of the hangar and releases it at the berth's entry
# about 700 ticks later, nose away from the yard, with the frigate below and behind on the left.
click tick=5 at=1720,232
# Throttle to four fifths, and at once a hard turn to the right: the cursor at the right edge, the
# left button held past the quarter-second that arms it. The turn clears the frigate's stern. The
# flight starts well after the release, so that a release a few ticks either way flies the same path.
key   tick=720 name=W ticks=48
move  tick=720 at=1900,540
key   tick=721 name=MouseLeft ticks=218
# The cursor below the centre: the nose goes down and round, half a loop, until the ship flies back
# level under the frigate, 115 m lower than it was released.
move  tick=765 at=960,916
move  tick=929 at=960,540
# Under the hull the throttle comes back to nothing, and the ship stops below the breach, inside its
# zone and clear of the hull above. The run ends there, the warning on the HUD.
key   tick=1008 name=S ticks=60
move  tick=1110 at=960,540
